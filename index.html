AdvantEdge Card Sign-Up Form Generator

AdvantEdge Card Numbers

(Print in landscape)

Instructions

  1. Get a stack of AdvantEdge cards and pre-activate them all in Loyalty as Instant Issue.
  2. Scan or type card numbers into the box on the left. Make sure each card number is on its own line.
  3. Click the "Generate" button to create a sign-up form for each card number.
  4. Click the "Print" button to print the forms. For best results, print in landscape.
  5. Cut out the printed forms then tape them to the actual cards.
  6. Give the forms to cashiers to sign them up at the register.
  7. Once a customer fills it out, collect it and enter the guest's information into Loyalty by end of day.
